# [No Man's Sky 3D Base Aligner](https://github.com/ebaleytherogue/No-Mans-Sky-3D-Base-Aligner) \- Use Cases

By ebaleytherogue

**A base’s position,  relative to its planet, can be adjusted. This is true for the entire base or just selected portions of the base.** And by ‘base’ I mean “the collection of base objects that make up a base”.

And remember, **all wiring (electrical, short-range teleporter, and supply pipes {which behave like wiring at the connection levels}) remain intact when using this app\!**

**Use Case:**

Fixing a Trading Post Base

I visited a base I’d built at a Trading Depot. The base objects that were on any of the depot’s platforms all “fell” to and into the ground beneath the depot. However, base part farther away \-- on the way to, and within, a nearby planetary archive \-- remained where I had placed them.

I needed a way to correct the positions of the base objects. And what’s more, I needed a way to leave the other objects where they were.

I created the functionality to do both of those things.

See [https\://www\.youtube.com/watch?v=1q\_TYTSEzFk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1q_TYTSEzFk) for a walkthrough of this functionality. The video pre-dates when I added full model support to the app. The interface shown is the HTML viewer and not the Windows app. Since this video was created I added a few more controls and tweaks to the app, but this workflow will work fine.

**Use Case:**

Moving part of a base from where it hangs in the sky, where a floating island USED to be.

The user can lower just that part of the base, moving it laterally over to another floating island. Or down to the ground. Or on top of the other part of the base. Wherever.

**Use Case:**

You’ve built an impressive base beside a crashed freighter. You think it would be cool to TILT the base to match the tilt of the freighter. In the app, you select only the portions of the base that you want to tilt, and then you tilt it in 3D space. You go into the game, look at it, make some mental notes, pop back into the tool, adjust it a bit. Soon you have the base placed where you wish.
